# M365 Copilot and Fabric Data Agent Questions

Use these in M365 Chat if your tenant has Fabric IQ/Data Agent integration enabled. Otherwise, ask the same questions in Fabric Data Agent.

## Executive questions

1. What are the top inventory risks this month?
2. Which product families need planner attention after the revised plan?
3. How much working capital does the revised plan add?
4. Which plant has the biggest gap between current inventory and planned demand?

## Planner questions

1. Which SKUs are still at stockout risk after the revised plan?
2. Show baseline demand, revised planned demand, reorder quantity, and projected inventory for high-risk SKUs.
3. Which supplier delays are creating the biggest planning risk?
4. Where did the revised plan reduce stockout risk the most?

## Sales-demo questions

1. Compare the current actuals, baseline plan, and revised plan for Electronics.
2. Explain why `MAT-400` at `PL-20` is a planning concern.
3. What should the planner do first to reduce stockout risk?
4. Summarize the revised plan in three bullets for a supply chain VP.

## Visual prompt ideas

1. Create a visual comparing stockout risk before and after the revised plan by product family.
2. Show working capital impact by plant and product family.
3. Plot available inventory, planned demand, and revised reorder quantity for the top five risk items.
